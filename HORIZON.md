# PROTOLITH — HORIZON (the speculative program)

*Companion to [DESIGN.md](DESIGN.md). Everything in this file is real thinking and none of it is committed.*

**Why this file exists.** v0.1 grew by three successive self-audits, each of which was intellectually honest and each of which *added* scope — the audits functioned as a one-way ratchet, and by the end a texture generator had become a research program terminating in GW+BSE calculations on corundum. The split puts the ratchet's output here, where it can keep growing without quietly reframing the buildable project as an unbounded one. DESIGN.md commits to M0–M2 and plans M3–M8; this file holds v0.1's §15–§19 (original section numbers preserved — cross-references to §0–§14 resolve into DESIGN.md) and milestones M9–M14.

Two items were already promoted in v0.2 because they were *corrections*, not scope: the §16.1 occupancy-isosurface fix (now DESIGN §2/§6 — it repaired a contradiction) and §16.2's τ(x) thermal fields (one line in DESIGN §5.1; the exotic applications stay here).

## 15. Second audit — the remaining dark corners

A hostile re-review of §14's coverage claim. The residual gaps fall into five families, and — remarkably — the largest one is not missing physics but missing *eyes*.

### 15.1 The compiler's missing eyes

Several "impossible" rocks turn out to be **fabric-covered already** and fail only at the output stage. **Labradorite** is the crown jewel: labradorescence comes from Bøggild exsolution lamellae in plagioclase — which `unmix` already generates, with C–H even predicting the lamellar wavelength λ*. Thin-film interference color is a function of exactly that spacing, so emitting a thin-film thickness + IOR channel (the KHR_materials_iridescence / OpenPBR convention) where lamellae exist yields **structural color computed from the simulated microstructure** — the schiller's hue is a first-principles output, not a paint job. Moonstone's adularescence (cryptoperthitic scattering) rides the same channel. **Opal** is `deposit` of monodisperse silica spheres in quiet water; packing-domain order gives Bragg diffraction — fabric machinery already exists, only the iridescence output was missing. **Chatoyancy** (tiger's eye, satin-spar silk, ribbon-mylonite sheen) needs tangent + anisotropy maps — and fibrous fabrics already *store* per-voxel fiber orientation; the compiler just never wrote it down. Cheap additions rounding out the eye: **emission** (blackbody T→color for molten/fresh-flow mode; a UV-fluorescence DB field for a lamp mode — calcite, fluorite, willemite), and the **wet variant** (albedo darkening + water-film F0), promoted from out-of-scope.

### 15.2 Fracture physics stage C doesn't have

The crack solver grows fractures from seeds; several iconic patterns need different mechanics. **Contraction cracking** — hierarchical energy-relaxation tiling with the documented T→Y junction maturation (Goehring & Morris) — covers mudcracks, columnar-basalt colonnade cross-sections, septarian interiors, and perlitic onion cracks in glass with one new crack mode. **Shock**: `shock[P]` as a radial conical fracture field plus local vitrification gives shatter cones (striated conical fractures, *the* diagnostic impact texture), suevite breccia, and tektite glass. **Pressure solution** is the anti-`precipitate`: stress-localized dissolution at grain contacts accumulating insoluble residue — stylolites, whose self-affine seam roughness is studied quantitatively in the physics literature and thus becomes another self-test. **Folding**: fabric-scale deform doesn't buckle layers; a displacement-field operator with viscosity-contrast buckling at the Biot dominant wavelength λ_d = 2πh(μ₁/6μ₂)^(1/3) gives folded marble and gneiss (crenulation cleavage, pleasingly, is already free: compose two `deform`s). **Soft-sediment deformation**: load casts and flame structures are Rayleigh–Taylor fingers from density inversion — a fluid-rheology `deform` variant; bioturbation is a stochastic rework pass on laminae.

### 15.3 Growth morphologies crystallize can't grow

The gauge produces compact convex crystals; fibrous mode covers spherulites; but **dendritic/skeletal growth** — the Mullins–Sekerka instability — is absent, and it owns some showpieces: manganese-oxide ferns on limestone, komatiite spinifex (skeletal olivine blades), hopper halite. The minimal model is **Laplacian growth** (DLA and the η-family) — and the same primitive, run as dielectric breakdown (DBM), traces the branching discharge path of a **fulgurite**, which is then just vitrify-along-path through deposited sand. One classic primitive, three phenomena. **Eutectic coupled growth** (graphic granite's runic quartz–feldspar intergrowth) is interface-coupled bi-phase crystallization, Jackson–Hunt style; v1 may approximate it with an unmix-like lamellar pattern, declared as such — mechanism debated in petrology anyway.

### 15.4 Environment regimes

Weathering chemistry is Earth-water-hardwired only in its *tables*. **Planetary regimes** are table swaps: lunar space weathering (nanophase-iron darkening/reddening, agglutinate glass welding — micrometeorite gardening as the moisture analogue), Mars oxidation. **Ice is a rock** and the verbs already know it: glaciologists describe glacier ice as metamorphic — snow→firn→ice is sintering, i.e., Potts coarsening; bubbles are vesicles; the blue is SSS. One DB entry plus a heavy translucency path.

### 15.5 Templates, and predictions the model owes us

Petrified wood is `precipitate` replacement acting on a wood-anatomy template (rings, rays, cell grid — content); stromatolites are accretion with a light-seeking rate function; concrete, brick, and slag are compositions of existing verbs wearing hard hats. Finally, the second audit surfaces **falsifiable predictions**: honeycomb **tafoni** should *emerge* from the existing moisture–AO feedback loop (shadowed hollows stay damp and weather faster — if honeycombs don't appear at the right parameters, the feedback is mis-tuned); porphyroblast growth under spin W should record **snowball-garnet inclusion trails**; stylolite roughness should reproduce its published scaling. A model that makes predictions is worth more than one that only makes textures. *(The tafoni prediction is what caught the v0.1 height-field bug — see §16.1, now fixed in DESIGN §2.)*

**Updated residue:** migmatite (unchanged), pahoehoe/aa surface fluid dynamics (ropy skin-buckling may partially reuse the fold operator; flagged partial), full Gibbs equilibria. Everything else above is scoped into M9–M10.

## 16. Third audit — solver taxonomy, a caught bug, and the long tail

**Taxonomy ruling.** Laplacian growth is a *solver*, not verb #7. Verbs are typed by their petrofabric semantics; Laplacian growth is invoked by three different verbs (dendritic `crystallize`, dendritic `precipitate`, DBM path-routing for fulgurites) — the signature of a shared computational primitive. The design therefore names its **solver library** explicitly: {JFA, RD ping-pong, Potts/Metropolis, Cahn–Hilliard, Laplacian growth}. Six verbs, five solvers.

### 16.1 A bug this audit caught — *promoted to DESIGN in v0.2*

§15 predicted tafoni should emerge from the moisture–AO feedback — but the original §6 surface was a single-valued height field z_top(x,y), **which cannot represent an overhang**. The model's own prediction didn't fit its data structure. Fix: the outcrop surface is the **voxel occupancy isosurface**; erosion and dissolution remove voxels, not height. The height *map* output becomes a projection of the isosurface (with AO carrying cavity information), and normals come from the occupancy gradient. Cost is modest — the slab was volumetric all along; only stage C/D's surface bookkeeping changes. Cavernous weathering, undercut ledges, and honeycomb cells are now representable, so the tafoni prediction is testable rather than vacuously false. **Status: applied throughout DESIGN §2/§6/§7/§8 as of v0.2.**

### 16.2 Thermal fields, not thermal scalars — *promoted to DESIGN in v0.2*

Cooling rate τ is a scalar per stage — but real igneous bodies have margins. Making τ a *field*, τ(x) = f(distance to contact), yields with one change: chilled dike margins, pillow-basalt glassy rinds with inward-coarsening interiors, contact-aureole grain-size gradients in hornfels, and finer porphyry rims. **Status: the mechanism is one line in DESIGN §5.1; the preset applications (pillow basalt, chilled-margin dike, hornfels aureole) remain here at M11.**

### 16.3 Sculpting the missing surfaces

Three surface-process families stage C/D lacked. **Flow-coupled dissolution**: thin-film water flow modulating dissolution rate produces scallops (whose wavelength inversely records flow speed — a paleoflow self-test), rillenkarren, and flutes; a free-boundary dissolution mode on the isosurface. **Directional abrasion**: erosion weighted by exposure to a wind/ice direction gives ventifact facets, desert polish, and glacial polish with striations (striae as directional scratch strokes). **Unloading cracks**: a surface-parallel fracture family (sheeting joints) completes exfoliation and spheroidal-weathering corestones, which alteration fronts alone could not finish. Related additions: efflorescence (salt precipitating at the evaporation front — the same haloclasty physics that drives tafoni), displacive growth (crystals pushing rather than replacing host: chicken-wire anhydrite, enterolithic folds), and **architectural/natural finishes** as a surface post-pass (polished / honed / flamed / sawn / bush-hammered; glacial and desert polish reuse it).

### 16.4 The instrument compiler

Reframe: PBR is *one compile target*. The petrofabric supports others nearly free — crossed-polarizer thin sections (DESIGN §10, already planned), UV fluorescence, **cathodoluminescence** (which distinguishes quartz overgrowths from detrital cores — a direct visual validation of the diagenesis stage), **BSE contrast** (mean atomic number, trivially computable from composition — SEM imagery for free), and thermal-IR emissivity (splib07 extends to 200 μm). One fabric, many instruments; each extra instrument is another axis on which the simulation can be checked against reality.

### 16.5 Chromophores, inclusions, films

Color *causes* the DB didn't model: **irradiation color centers** (smoky quartz tinted in proportion to dose from neighboring U/Th-bearing accessories — spatially causal color, a provenance-consistent detail); Cr³⁺/Fe²⁺–Ti⁴⁺ variety spectra as DB entries (ruby, sapphire, emerald-green minerals). **Fluid inclusions**: vein quartz is milky *because* fast growth traps micro-inclusions that scatter — one crystallize parameter (growth rate → inclusion density → scattering) explains white veins vs clear rock crystal. **Tarnish**: bornite/chalcopyrite peacock iridescence is a thin oxidation film — free once the §15 thin-film channel exists, driven by the weathering clock. **Reaction rims**: pairwise boundary reactions (mineral A|B → corona C) via a boundary-detect + grow pass — kelyphite around garnet, myrmekite fringes. **Oscillatory zoning** may emerge by coupling growth to local melt depletion (optional, self-organization welcome). **Biological films**: Tintenstriche — the dark cyanobacterial drainage streaks on cliff faces — are geometrically identical to the Fe-stain advection already built; a second source term and palette.

### 16.6 Scale and emission

Above the slab: a lightweight **structural layer** (joint sets, bedding orientation, fold trains) that spatially modulates slab parameters, so cliff-scale texturing composes from hand-sample physics; also a fabric-vs-slab rotation so cuts can cross foliation at any angle. Below the texel: **self-calibrating roughness** — run a small high-resolution patch per (mineral, state), prefilter its normal distribution LEAN/LEADR-style, and *derive* the roughness values DESIGN §8.3 currently hand-sets. Output-side: emit **Wang tile sets** by constraining JFA/CA boundary conditions per edge class, giving non-repeating infinite coverage from one program (the fabric-matching constraint is a boundary condition, not a post-process); and since every stage is time-stepped, **aging keyframes** (and wet/snow/frost transient states) export nearly free.

### 16.7 Inverse petrology

The calibration story becomes a harness: fit program parameters to reference photographs by black-box search (CMA-ES; the forward model runs in seconds — on the CUDA build) against texture-statistic + color losses (Portilla–Simoncelli / Gram features, spectra-aware color histograms, height-spectrum slope). This yields presets-from-photos, priors over rock programs, and the strongest validation available: *can the model reach reality, and with geologically sensible parameters when it does?*

### 16.8 Database tiering and residue

The mineral DB roadmaps from 16 to ~60 in visual-leverage tiers (tier 2 urgently wants garnet, chlorite, epidote, serpentine, glauconite, zeolites for amygdales, chalcopyrite/bornite, galena, sphalerite, graphite, gypsum/anhydrite, halite, fluorite, barite, chromite, siderite, corundum, diamond, rutile, spinel). **Residue after three audits:** migmatite (unchanged), pahoehoe/aa surface fluid dynamics (partial — ropy skins may reuse the fold operator), full Gibbs equilibria, and a shrinking but honestly nonzero set of unknown unknowns.

## 17. Single crystals and gems — Lapidary mode

"Does this cover ruby and diamond?" splits into two questions.

**Gem-bearing rocks: yes, mostly already.** Mogok-type ruby is corundum porphyroblasts grown during metamorphism of impure carbonate — a program the algebra already writes: `deform(deposit(carbonate + Al,Cr impurities))` with corundum in the porphyroblast set. Diamond's host needs one composition: **kimberlite** is a volatile-driven breccia pipe — xenoliths and serpentinized olivine macrocrysts in a fine matrix, i.e., `deposit`-style clast machinery inside a `crystallize` groundmass, with diamond as a xenocryst clast species. Corundum, diamond, rutile, and spinel join the DB (§16.8). Diamond's entry is fun on its own: n = 2.417 gives F0 ≈ 0.17 — adamantine luster is a *number* — and resorption in the kimberlite magma rounds octahedra toward dodecahedroids, which is single-crystal dissolution, machinery we have.

**Gems as objects: a genuine representational boundary — the deepest "not covered" of all four audits.** PBR texture maps describe surface reflectance of effectively opaque materials. A cut gem's appearance — brilliance, fire, scintillation, color saturation that varies with ray path length — is volumetric spectral light transport through a transparent single crystal: refraction, dispersion, total internal reflection, pleochroic absorption. No texture map can hold it; this is a limit of the *output representation*, not of the physics stages.

**But the fabric machinery covers gem interiors almost completely**, which motivates a single-crystal mode: one seed, unimpeded gauge growth, history recorded volumetrically. Then, delightfully: **sector zoning falls out of the gauge argmax** — γ_P already computes which habit face claims each voxel, and that face identity *is* the growth sector (amethyst's sector-zoned color, diamond's cuboctahedral CL sectors — visible under the §16.4 CL instrument — for free). Growth/color zoning from shell time (sapphire's hexagonal banding); rutile silk via the fibrous solver along crystallographic axes — three directions at 60° in corundum's basal plane — which the anisotropy channel turns into **asterism** when the virtual cut is a cabochon (star ruby as an emergent consequence of two existing subsystems); "fingerprints" as crack + `precipitate` healing; trigons as etch dissolution on octahedral faces; §16.5 chromophores supply Cr³⁺ ruby red, N-yellow, B-blue, and irradiation-green diamond. And under the existing UV instrument mode, **ruby glows red for free** — Cr³⁺ R-line emission at 694 nm, the ruby-laser transition, already implied by the fluorescence DB field.

**Compile target #7: the gem renderer.** Output a volumetric optical description — spectral absorption field κ(λ, x), IOR + birefringence tensor, inclusion list — plus facet geometry (standard cut libraries or rough habit), consumed by a small spectral refractive path tracer with dispersion (fire) and, ideally, polarization (pleochroism: ruby's o/e dichroism). A spectral tracer for a convex faceted solid is a contained, tractable rendering project.

Scoped as **M13 — Lapidary**: single-crystal mode, gem DB entries, the volumetric gem target + spectral tracer → ruby (with silk, fingerprints, star cabochon), sapphire (zoned), amethyst (sector-zoned), diamond (sectors under CL, trigons, fancy colors), emerald (jardin = healed-fracture gardens).

## 18. The optical volume — demoting PBR from canon to projection

Freed from the texture-map assumption, the right canonical representation is: **the slab as a piece of virtual matter** — occupancy plus spectral, anisotropic optical-coefficient fields — with every output, PBR included, a *projection* of it.

**The representation.** Per voxel, derived on demand from (mineral_id, orientation, alteration) + the DB — nothing new is stored; the petrofabric *is* the compression: spectral absorption κ(λ,x); scattering σ_s(λ,x) with phase function (fluid inclusions, micro-porosity); the **dielectric tensor** ε(λ,x) — per-mineral principal indices (uniaxial: calcite, quartz, corundum; biaxial: feldspars, olivine) rotated by the stored quaternion, so birefringence, pleochroism, and optic-axis behavior come from data the fabric already carries; emission response (fluorescence excitation→emission pairs, blackbody for melt). Appearance is then *never authored*: it is the solution of light transport through this medium.

**The unification.** Opacity is the high-absorption *limit* of this representation; PBR surface maps were always a boundary-layer approximation valid when the mean free path is far below feature scale. One dial — optical depth — spans the whole project: diamond (ballistic + refraction), marble (diffusive), granite (opaque boundary). Gems, SSS, virtual thin sections, UV/CL instruments, and rasterizer texture maps stop being separate channels and become **transport regimes and response functions of one object**. §17's gem tracer generalizes into *the* renderer; Lapidary is its ballistic corner. Gems are in fact the renderer's easy case — homogeneous interior, facet boundary, sparse inclusion list — while granite is the case that justifies baking, since rays die within microns of the surface.

**Two-scale honesty.** Geometric transport is the backbone; where fabric scale approaches λ, local wave-optical response models take over with parameters *taken from the simulated fabric*: thin-film matrices on unmix lamellae (labradorite), Bragg response on opal's sphere packing, diffraction on fine laminae. §15.1's iridescence channel is retroactively revealed as this hybrid, now stated as principle rather than patch.

**Polarization plan.** v1 tracks the two eigenpolarizations through birefringent media (sufficient for pleochroism, gem dichroism, and XPL thin sections); full Mueller-matrix transport is the declared upgrade if XPL fidelity demands it.

**PBR, demoted and improved.** The DESIGN §8 direct compile survives as the *fast opaque-limit projection*. Its principled successor is **fitted PBR**: render the optical volume under a probe rig and fit microfacet parameters per texel — the texture maps become *measurements of a virtual sample*, self-consistent with the volume by construction. We stop painting rocks and start photographing virtual ones.

**DB upgrades required.** Sellmeier n(λ) coefficients per mineral (dispersion — diamond's fire is a Sellmeier curve, not a scalar); *transmission/absorption* spectra for transparent minerals (diffuse-reflectance data like splib07 is the right source for opaque projection but converts to κ only approximately, via Kubelka–Munk — the honest note is to source both where available); fluorescence excitation–emission pairs; tensor symmetry class.

**Engineering.** Hero-wavelength spectral Monte Carlo over voxel DDA traversal; refractive events at the occupancy boundary and at inclusion interfaces; diffusion approximation as an accelerator in the marble regime. M13 accordingly generalizes from "gem tracer" to "optical-volume renderer," with the PBR bake, thin-section, instrument, and Lapidary targets as its projection heads.

## 19. The ab initio basement — how far first principles reach

The DB's optics column is not fundamental; every entry is a view of one master object, the complex dielectric function ε(ω), and much of it is computable from the crystal structure alone. This section records how deep the derivation goes, where it stops, and what the pipeline does about it.

**Dispersion is the shadow of absorption.** Kramers–Kronig ties n(λ) to the absorption spectrum over all frequencies: the Sellmeier poles are not fitted mysteries but literally the UV electronic and IR phonon resonances. Compute the absorption structure and dispersion follows — diamond's fire is a property of its band structure. Computing ε with the crystal's actual symmetry yields the *tensor*, i.e., birefringence ab initio.

**The method ladder** is mature: DFT gives band structure but underestimates gaps by ~30–50%; GW corrects quasiparticle gaps to ~0.1–0.3 eV; BSE adds excitonic effects, which matter substantially in wide-gap insulators like most minerals. GW+BSE is the gold standard for crystal optical spectra; DFPT supplies the phonon side — IR response and reststrahlen bands, which is the §16.4 TIR emissivity channel from first principles. Honest accuracy: computed n typically lands within a few percent — which sounds excellent until noted that 2% of quartz's 1.55 is Δn ≈ 0.03, visually meaningful for dispersion; and birefringence, being a difference of two large numbers, is the hardest optical target. Good, not gemologically calibrated.

**Color is defect physics**, and that field received an enormous windfall from quantum-computing research: the NV center made diamond point defects the most-calculated objects in condensed matter, so diamond's fancy colors are the best-computed colors of anything. The showpiece: the **ruby/emerald puzzle is solved ab initio** — the same Cr³⁺ ion is red in corundum and green in beryl purely from crystal-field differences at the substitution site, reproduced by embedded-cluster multireference calculations; even the pressure shift of ruby's R-line (the fluorescence gauge used inside diamond anvil cells — ruby measuring pressure inside diamond) has been computed. Weakest link: blue sapphire's Fe²⁺–Ti⁴⁺ intervalence charge transfer, a strongly correlated pair problem where calculation still delivers mechanism more than quantitative spectrum.

**Already first-principles inside this pipeline, no quantum mechanics required:** scattering. Milky quartz and opal fire are Mie theory applied to the *simulated* inclusion and sphere populations — sizes and Δn come from the fabric, σ_s and the phase function follow analytically. This replaces the DB `scatter` field wherever the fabric resolves the scatterers, is independent of the quantum ladder, and folds directly into M13's renderer.

**The gradient across the rest of the DB:** density and elastic constants are routine (~1%); cleavage planes fall out of ab initio surface energies; habit polytopes come from Wulff/attachment-energy constructions (pharmaceutical crystallization predicts shapes this way as a matter of course); hardness is derivable-ish via elastic-modulus correlations. Then the wall: **weathering rate constants**. DFT plus transition-state theory yields mechanisms and activation energies, but field dissolution rates span orders of magnitude with pH, defect density, and biology — kinetics stays empirical, sourced from standard lab compilations (e.g., the USGS Palandri–Kharaka rate parameters). Optics goes all the way down; mechanics most of the way; kinetics stays measured.

**Pragmatic plan.** Every DB optical entry carries a **provenance tag** — measured | computed | hybrid — so both value systems coexist and disagree visibly. Default remains measured data. The computed path: pull Materials Project DFPT dielectric tensors as priors for all ~60 minerals, scissor-correct gaps against a few well-measured anchors, and run a pilot GW+BSE campaign on the visually critical set (quartz, calcite, corundum, diamond) — a bounded HPC project, not a moonshot. The payoff is an inversion of the data flow: splib07 moves from *input* to *validation*, and the stack reads Schrödinger → ε(ω) → fabric → light. Ship-the-cause bottoms out at the Hamiltonian; the mineral DB was never data, just cache.

## Horizon milestones (M9–M14)

**M9: fracture-physics pack** (contraction cracking, shock, pressure solution, folding, soft-sediment) → columnar basalt, septaria, shatter-cone impactite, stylolitic limestone, folded marble, load-cast sandstone. **M10: Laplacian growth + extended optics channels** (iridescence, anisotropy, emission, fluorescence, wet) + planetary tables + templates → labradorite, moonstone, opal, tiger's eye, komatiite, Mn-dendrite limestone, fulgurite, petrified wood, stromatolite, chondrite, pallasite, glacier ice, lunar regolith. The gallery grows toward ~45 presets. **M11: isosurface-native surface processes** — flow-sculpted dissolution, directional abrasion, unloading cracks, finishes — plus the τ(x) preset family → tafoni test, pillow basalt, chilled-margin dike, scalloped limestone, ventifact, exfoliating granite dome, polished/flamed slabs. **M12: instrument targets** (UV, CL, BSE, TIR) + chromophore/inclusion/tarnish pack + reaction rims + structural layer + Wang tile emission + NDF-derived roughness + inverse-petrology harness → smoky pegmatite, milky quartz vein, peacock bornite, corona gabbro, and presets fitted directly from photographs. **M13 (optical volume / Lapidary):** the spectral transport renderer over derived coefficient fields, with projection heads for gems (ballistic), marble (diffusive), fitted-PBR bake (opaque), polarized thin sections, and instrument modes → ruby, star ruby, zoned sapphire, sector-zoned amethyst, fancy-color diamonds, emerald with jardin, and PBR maps that are photographs of virtual samples. **M14 (ab initio basement, optional):** provenance-tagged DB + Materials Project dielectric-tensor priors + Mie-from-fabric scattering + pilot GW+BSE set (quartz, calcite, corundum, diamond) → splib07 graduates from input to validation.
